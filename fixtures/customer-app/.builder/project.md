# Project

A small application where people sign up, work in teams and manage who is in
each team.

## People

- **Owner**: created the team; can do everything in it.
- **Admin**: can rename the team, change members' roles and remove members.
- **Member**: belongs to the team and can see it.

## Terms

- A **team** is a group of people working together. Everyone gets a personal
  team when they sign up.
