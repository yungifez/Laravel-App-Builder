# Terms

Last changed 3 October 2026.

These terms apply when you use this product to make and change apps.

## Your apps are yours

The code of every app you make here belongs to you. You can download it at any
time and run it anywhere.

## Using the product

- Keep your account to yourself, and keep your password safe.
- Do not use the product to make apps that break the law, harm people, or
  attack other systems.
- Do not try to get around plan limits or reach other people's apps.

We may stop an account that breaks these rules.

## Plans and payment

- The Free plan costs nothing. Paid plans are billed each month through Stripe.
- Each plan includes an amount of AI use a month. When it is used up, new
  changes wait until it starts again or you move to a bigger plan.
- You can change or cancel your plan at any time in Settings → Plan and use.
  A cancelled plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.

## What we promise, and what we do not

Every change is checked before you see it, and you decide what to keep. Checks
can still miss things, so try each change before you rely on it. The product is
provided as it is, without other promises.

## Changes to these terms

When these terms change, we update the date at the top and tell you by email
about anything that affects you.
