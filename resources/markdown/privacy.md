# Privacy

Last changed 3 October 2026.

This page says what we keep about you, why, and who else sees it.

## What we keep

- **Your account:** your name, your email address and your password, which we
  store only in a form that cannot be read back.
- **Your apps:** the code of each app you make or bring, every change you ask
  for, and the pictures you attach to a request.
- **How your changes went:** what each change did, the checks that ran on it,
  and what each AI call for your apps cost us.
- **Messages you send us** through the contact form.
- **Billing:** if you pay for a plan, Stripe keeps your card. We keep only a
  reference to your Stripe customer, your card's type and last four digits,
  and your plan.

## Who else sees it

- **AI providers.** To plan, write and check a change, we send your request and
  the parts of your app's code it needs to Anthropic or OpenAI.
- **Stripe** handles payments for paid plans.
- **Our email provider** sends the emails we send you.

We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

## Cookies

We set only the cookies the product needs: one to keep you signed in, one to
protect forms, and a few that remember your choices, such as light or dark
mode and your time zone. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

## Your choices

- Download the code of any of your apps at any time.
- Delete your account in Settings → Profile. This deletes your apps and their
  history from the product.
- Write to us through the contact page with any question about your data.
